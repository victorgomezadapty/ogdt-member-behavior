Aggregate tables behind every chart on ogdt-member-behavior.

survival_*.csv and days_without_visit_at_end.csv: derived from the Lisboa Sport Services Dataset
(Pinheiro & Cavique, Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/yprk4jdgnv.1), CC BY 4.0.
weekly_*.csv: derived from the replication data of Carrera, Royer, Stehr, Sydnor & Taubinsky,
Who Chooses Commitment? (Review of Economic Studies), Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.5173081, data under CC0 1.0.

Survival values are Kaplan-Meier estimates with log-log 95% intervals (Greenwood variance).
Persistence intervals are percentile intervals from 1,000 person-level bootstrap resamples (seed 20261110).
These tables are shared under CC BY 4.0 with the attribution above.
